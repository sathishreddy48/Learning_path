Read this first: these are moving targets

Loop structures, assessment formats, level ladders, cooldown periods and compensation bands all differ between companies and change over time — sometimes between two teams at the same company in the same year. Everything here is the shape to expect and the method for handling it, not a current specification. Your recruiter is the authoritative source for your own process, and they will tell you if you ask. The pay figures on the offer page are illustrative arithmetic, not benchmarks; get real ranges from levels.fyi and from people at that company and level.

How to use this site
  • Read The Loop first if you are already preparing — it tells you which of the four technical tracks your level is actually graded on, which is the thing most likely to be misallocating your time right now.
  • Read Levels early if you have not yet applied. Targeting the wrong rung is expensive in both directions and it is largely under your control.
  • The Twelve-Week Plan is the scheduling layer over the other four sites. Take its ratios and adjust them to your level.
  • Interview angle boxes here say what the stage is really assessing, and the thing to do about it.
  • Visited state lives in your browser only, separate from the C#, System Design, DSA and LLD sites.
